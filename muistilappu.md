U1 — Unconsolidated, full accounts
The company's own standalone financials only — no subsidiaries included. Full disclosure meaning complete P&L and balance sheet. This is what you want for most Finnish private companies. A company with 3 subsidiaries shows only the parent entity's own activity.
U2 — Unconsolidated, limited accounts
Standalone financials but with limited disclosure — fewer line items reported. Common for smaller Finnish firms that meet the minimum filing threshold but aren't required to file full accounts. You'll have more missing values in U2 companies.
C1 — Consolidated, full accounts
The parent company plus all subsidiaries combined into one set of accounts. Full disclosure. This is what Nokia, Neste, Kesko file — their accounts represent the entire group globally.
C2 — Consolidated, limited accounts
Consolidated but with limited disclosure. Rare in your dataset.
